# Scene 1

[A closed repair shop. Rain taps an empty flowerpot.]

ADA: You left the light on.

BEA: I left two lights on. One of them has made a decision.

[Ada sets a paper bag on the counter. Something inside clicks.]

ADA: Before you ask, it isn't a clock.

BEA: I wasn't going to ask until you said that.

ADA: Then let's both enjoy the remaining mystery.

# Scene 2

[Bea opens the bag. A wind-up pigeon tips onto its side.]

BEA: Your customer wants this repaired?

ADA: My customer wants it to stop proposing to the toaster.

[Bea rights the pigeon. It rolls toward the dark lamp.]

BEA: Perhaps its tastes have changed.

ADA: Perhaps we should charge for the consultation.

# Scene 3

[The pigeon bumps the lamp switch. Both lights shine.]

BEA: There. An electrician.

ADA: Do we pay it?

BEA: We offer benefits. A dry shelf. No magnets.

[Ada folds the empty bag and puts it in her pocket.]

ADA: Leave both lights on tonight.
