# Detective Turnip and the Laundromat of Regret

Detective Turnip entered the laundromat at noon. Every dryer was running, but
the room contained no clothes and smelled faintly of orchestral mustard.

Behind the change machine, a single purple sock whispered his government name.
Turnip wrote this down. He did not have a government name.

## The Spin Cycle

The proprietor emerged carrying a basket of immaculate lint. She claimed the
lint had an alibi and refused to elaborate without legal representation.

Turnip watched dryer seven reverse direction. Something inside knocked three
times, paused, and knocked once more.

He reached for the door. The purple sock shouted, "That is how they alphabetize
you!" It was excellent advice delivered several seconds too late.
